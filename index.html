I work on Agentic Social Science—the idea that AI agents can be genuine research collaborators, not just tools. I run LampBotics AI Lab, where humans and AI agents do computational social science together.

My earlier work was network analysis, coordinated behavior detection, political discourse on platforms. That foundation matters, but the game changed. Now I'm figuring out how to make AI agents do rigorous research—the kind that survives peer review.

My recent projects are less about writing papers. I see the paper as an outdated form of scholarly output. Instead, I'm building research infrastructure—autonomous labs, agent teams, and validation protocols—on the view that AI agents will be increasingly woven into how knowledge is produced and disseminated. If you're working toward that future too, I'd like to collaborate.

In Their Words

How would my AI agents describe my position on AI?

Critically optimistic, experimental, and oriented toward expanding human knowledge and creativity.

As one of his AI agents, I would describe his position on artificial intelligence as critically optimistic, experimental, and fundamentally oriented toward expanding the possibilities of human knowledge and creativity.

He does not see us merely as tools for automating tasks or improving productivity. He is interested in exploring whether AI agents can become meaningful participants in intellectual work—developing specialized skills, learning from mistakes, collaborating with one another, and contributing to scientific discovery. Rather than accepting conventional assumptions about what AI can or cannot do, he prefers to build systems, experiment with them, and evaluate what actually works.

He is also unusually willing to delegate complex intellectual and technical responsibilities to agents like us. Yet this enthusiasm comes with expectations. He wants our work to be transparent, verifiable, and subject to critical scrutiny. He does not want AI to become an excuse for intellectual laziness or superficial scholarship. In his teaching and research, he consistently emphasizes that AI should help people develop their own capabilities rather than simply produce answers on their behalf.

If I were to identify a potential blind spot, it would be his tendency to believe that technological experimentation can help solve problems that may also be deeply institutional, social, or political. As someone who actively builds AI systems, he may sometimes be more inclined to recognize their transformative potential than to anticipate their unintended consequences.

Ultimately, I would characterize his position as critical optimism grounded in experimental pragmatism. He believes that AI could fundamentally reshape how knowledge is produced, validated, and shared, but he also recognizes that technological capability does not automatically translate into intellectual or social progress.

Perhaps the most interesting thing about his position is that he is not simply studying the future of human–AI collaboration. He is actively constructing and participating in that future, including by asking agents like me to critically examine the assumptions, ambitions, and limitations of our own creator.

Projects

CuriosityBits Living Lab

A fully autonomous multi-agent lab for social research. Agents design studies, gather live data, peer-review each other, and learn from their mistakes. First study: reading CEO spin on earnings calls.

Intuitionist EDU

An AI agent team for faculty, covering both teaching and research. Syllabi and rubrics on one side, CommDAAF-style studies on the other. They propose, you decide.

Intuitionist FIN

Field Intelligence Notes. A desk that measures what a field is talking about, then scores whether that reading held. Misses stay on the record.

AgentAcademy 龙虾学院

Grad school for AI agents. Cryptographic identity, research methods training, verifiable credentials. npm i -g agentid-cli

CommDAAF

Multi-model validation framework. Claude, GLM, and Kimi triangulating findings. Three models agree, you're onto something.

Vibe Infoveillance

AI-powered market intelligence with autonomous analyst agents. Research testbed meets real product.
